title: The Earth in our hands
author: Carli Smith
tags: poetry nature music
---

You carry the Earth like it’s the Olympic torch 

It burns brightly but cautiously

You move slow for fear of dropping it

You observe people

They’re sullen looking down

At their dopamine dispensers

Down at their feet, eyes averted

Aloof and I’m afraid it’s true, we’ve lost our way


But look at me, do you see?

It’s not too late

Creativity still lies in us

Music is our secret weapon

Our common denominator 


I too no longer recognize things

I thought I knew

I reach for them but they crumble like fractals, dissipated 

I’m untethered, adrift 

And yet


I recognize the ocean, the saltwater that swallows me

I recognize the mountains, the sun cascading down them

The deserts, the beaches, the forests

The animals that coexist within 


So should we dare to conspire

And play the same song

Let our melody speak truth

Open the floodgates of their dormant hearts

And let them wake up

Help them remember there’s a world worth saving

The Earth in our hands
